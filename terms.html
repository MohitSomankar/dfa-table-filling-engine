Purpose

Table-Filling DFA Engine is an educational tool built by Mohit, B.Tech CSE (AI and ML), S.B. Jain Institute of Technology, Management and Research, Nagpur, for a Theory of Computation seminar. It shows how the table-filling algorithm finds the equivalence classes of a DFA.

Limits

The app accepts complete DFAs with up to 15 states and 5 single-character symbols. Input outside these limits is rejected with an explanation.
