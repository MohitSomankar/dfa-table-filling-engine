Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026.

Table-Filling DFA Engine is a student project for the Theory of Computation course. It works entirely inside your browser.

What the app does with your input

The DFA tables and strings you type are processed on your device. They are not sent to any server, and the app has no accounts, forms that submit data, analytics, advertising or cookies.

What is stored on your device

  • Your light or dark theme choice, in the browser's local storage.
  • A copy of the app files, kept by the service worker so the app can open offline. You can remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing site data in your browser.

Hosting

The site is delivered by a static hosting provider, which may keep ordinary server logs such as IP address and time of request, under its own policy. The app itself does not read or use those logs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
